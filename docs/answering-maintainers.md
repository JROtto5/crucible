# Answering maintainers on an upstream PR

1. **Answer every comment**, even if only with "Done in `abc1234`." Don't resolve a maintainer's thread yourself unless the project asks contributors to.
2. **Lead with the answer, then the evidence**: the commit, test name, benchmark or link. Keep it to a few sentences.
3. **If you disagree, say why once, with evidence,** and offer to do it their way. It's their project.
4. **Don't argue taste.** Style and naming requests are free to accept.
5. **Push fixes as new commits during review** unless they ask for a squash, and say which commit answers which comment.
6. **If a request is out of scope**, offer a follow-up issue or PR instead of growing this one.
7. **When you change behaviour, update the tests and the PR description** to match.
8. **Re-run the project's checks before saying "done".** Never claim CI passes before it has.
9. **Be patient.** One polite ping after a week or two is fine; more is not.
10. **Disclose AI assistance if the project's policy asks for it**, and know your change well enough to explain any line without the tool.
